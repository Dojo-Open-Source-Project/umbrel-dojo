Pair a wallet

Each wallet gets its own key, so losing one phone means revoking one key rather than re-pairing everything.

An expiry is a backstop, not a schedule: a key that lapses stops a wallet syncing until you pair it again. Ten years unless you have a reason to want it shorter.

This is your Dojo's Tor address. It works from anywhere, not just at home, and keeps the connection private. Your wallet needs Tor enabled.

Pair over your local network instead

Faster, and it works before Tor has published — but a wallet stores one address, and this one only resolves on your home network. A wallet paired this way will stop reaching your Dojo the moment you leave, and the traffic is not encrypted. Prefer the Tor code above unless you have a reason not to, such as reaching this Umbrel over your own VPN.

Ashigaru Wallet (Android)
  1. Install Ashigaru Wallet and choose Connect to existing Dojo during setup.
  2. Tap the QR scanner and scan the code above.
  3. Finish wallet creation. Ashigaru shows a green indicator once it is talking to your Dojo.
Samourai Wallet (Android)
  1. During setup choose Connect to existing Dojo, or from an existing wallet open Settings › Transactions › Dojo.
  2. Tap the QR scanner and scan the code above.
  3. The Dojo indicator turns solid once the wallet is talking to your node.
Sentinel (watch-only)
  1. Open Sentinel and go to Settings › Connect to Dojo.
  2. Scan the code above.
